BookIt

Book shared lab equipment without the clash.

BookIt replaces the paper sign-up sheet on the lab door. See what's free right now, drag across a calendar to book a slot, and let the system catch double-bookings, uncertified users and overdue returns.

  • Week calendar: drag across it to book
  • Live conflict check while you choose a slot
  • Safety-certification gate for restricted equipment
  • Utilisation reports, peak-hour heatmap, CSV export
Django · Django REST Framework · MySQL · Vanilla JS
